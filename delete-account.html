Delete Your MilkDigi Account

MilkDigi users can request deletion of their account and associated data by following the instructions below.

Account deletion requests:
Email: milkdigi.support@gmail.com

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to milkdigi.support@gmail.com.
  2. Send the request from the email address registered with your MilkDigi account whenever possible.
  3. Use the subject: MilkDigi Account Deletion Request.
  4. Include your registered MilkDigi email address in your request.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After a valid account deletion request is verified, the account and associated cloud data will be deleted, including applicable:

  • MilkDigi account information
  • Customer records
  • Milk delivery records
  • Milk quantity and rate information
  • Monthly billing records
  • Payment-related records stored in MilkDigi
  • Business profile information
  • Other account-associated cloud data stored by MilkDigi

Data Retention

Some information may be retained when required by applicable law, regulatory requirements, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Data Stored on Your Device

MilkDigi may store application data locally on your device to support offline functionality. After your account deletion request has been completed, you can remove remaining local application data by clearing the MilkDigi app data or uninstalling the app from your device.
